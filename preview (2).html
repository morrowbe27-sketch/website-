Healthy Food Choices

A balanced diet includes fruits, vegetables, whole grains, protein, and healthy fats. Teens should avoid too much fast food and sugary drinks because they can lead to low energy and health problems.

Sample Meal Ideas

  • Breakfast: Oatmeal with fruit
  • Lunch: Turkey sandwich with vegetables
  • Dinner: Grilled chicken with rice and salad
  • Snack: Yogurt and berries

Benefits of Balanced Eating

Balanced eating helps maintain energy levels, supports growth, improves concentration, and strengthens the immune system.
