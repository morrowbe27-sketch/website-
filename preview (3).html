Workout Ideas for Beginners

Exercise keeps the body healthy and strong. Teens should try to stay active for at least 60 minutes each day.

  • Walking or jogging
  • Yoga
  • Bodyweight exercises
  • Cycling
  • Dancing

Benefits of Exercise

Exercise improves mood, strengthens muscles, boosts confidence, and helps reduce stress.

Weekly Workout Schedule

  • Monday: Cardio
  • Tuesday: Strength Training
  • Wednesday: Yoga
  • Thursday: Walking
  • Friday: Dance Workout
  • Saturday: Outdoor Sports
  • Sunday: Rest Day
