Stress Management Tips

School, friendships, and social media can create stress for teens. Healthy stress management techniques include deep breathing, journaling, listening to music, and talking to trusted people.

Importance of Sleep

Sleep helps the brain and body recover. Teens should aim for 8–10 hours of sleep each night.

Self-Care Habits

  • Take breaks when needed
  • Spend time outdoors
  • Practice gratitude
  • Stay connected with friends and family
  • Limit screen time before bed
